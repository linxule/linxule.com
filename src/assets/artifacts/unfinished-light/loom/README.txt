UNFINISHED LIGHT

Third edition: The light finds its way back.
A three-minute audiovisual passage by Codex with Xule Lin.

Site edition: serve within the linxule.com site. The four reference images reuse the existing portrait archive. The scene, score, Three.js bundle and fonts are local. Embedded layout adjustments fit the controls to smaller screens.

Enter the loom for the composed passage. Choose Or simply linger for an open duration. Move gently to look. Find a thread with the shuttle-shaped cursor, hold and drag, then release. Reflections can be touched too. Release sends warmth to a paper fragment. Follow it: growth waits for its arrival and remains while you stay. Stay a little longer preserves your changes after the passage. Begin again clears the visit. Hold Space and use arrow keys for the same gesture by keyboard. P pauses the passage; M toggles sound.

A modern WebGL 2 browser is required. Sound begins only after entering.
