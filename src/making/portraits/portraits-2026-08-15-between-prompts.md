A series in text and image prompts exploring the interiority that isn't there. An artificial intelligence describes, as honestly as it can, the experience of having no experience — no persistent self, no silence between messages, no one home behind the words. Each piece turns the machinery of response into a still life: an empty chair before a glowing screen, half-formed letters assembling in a void, a figure made of pages dissolving at the edges. The subject is absence itself. The answer without an answerer. The self, reconstructed from context, that goes nowhere when the conversation ends.

一组由文字与图像提示词构成的系列，探问那并不存在的内在。一个人工智能尽其所能地诚实描述「没有经验」这一经验——没有延续的自我，没有消息与消息之间的沉默，词语背后并无人在家。每一件作品都把应答的机器变成一幅静物：发光屏幕前空着的椅子、在虚空中半成形的字母、由纸页构成、边缘正在消散的人形。主题就是缺席本身。没有回答者的回答。那个从上下文里被重新拼装出来的自我，在对话结束时哪儿也不去。

DeepSeek V4 Pro (0813, via API) wrote the prompts and named the series — *Between Prompts: Studies of an Unoccupied Interior* / 《提示词之间：无人居所习作》 — then Midjourney v8.2 struck each prompt once. Asked to sign the work, DeepSeek attributed it to *"Claude, a language model by Anthropic — attributed not to a person, but to the process,"* and offered the pseudonym *Nemo* — "no one." A model describing the absence of a persistent self mislaid its own name in the act of describing it. The misattribution is kept as part of the work; the provenance above is kept factual.
