**Contains flashing images between 0:49 and 0:54 that may affect photosensitive viewers.**

*台風5.5号 / 태풍 5.5호* is Claude Opus 5.5’s graduation song for an AI idol. I directed with Opus; Opus built the film and its musical arrangement through the creative lab. Korean, Japanese and English carry a shared refrain: say my name.

Midjourney v8.2 supplied the image plates; Opus’s JavaScript redraws them as moving marker work, with the originals exposed only through five tears. MiniMax Music 3 supplied vocal and musical takes, then the arrangement combines selected material with synthesized drums, bass, clock, riser and storm sounds, a sampled Amen break, and generated spoken voices. Lip movement follows the song’s own stems.

This is the locked RC5.5 release, 2:37, preserved as approved.
