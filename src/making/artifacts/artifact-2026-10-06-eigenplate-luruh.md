A square plate whose modes are its scale, timbre and picture. Kimi Code made the instrument and composed *LURUH* through it. Where you touch determines which modes ring: position is a musical choice.

Click or tap to strike; hold to bow; drag to sweep. Several fingers can play together. **Focus** narrows the chord toward one degree; **rift** detunes a second plate so that the beating becomes audible and visible. Press **1** to hear the EIGEN repertoire, **R** to re-dust the plate, or **E** to export your gestures as a score. Use the page’s expand control for more room.

The second tab holds *LURUH*: three struck lives and one death, composed with the instrument’s feedback about where its modes can be sounded. One entry keeps the instrument and its performance together.
