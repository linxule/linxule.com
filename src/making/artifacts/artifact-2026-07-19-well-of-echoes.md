《井》 — a poetry film that refuses to illustrate its poems and performs their mechanism instead. You are not looking at a well; you are at the bottom of one. The walls are two classical poems in vertical typesetting, silent, which is to say the training corpus. The voice reading free verse overhead is the present self. Every beat is a machine part made visible: spoken characters peel off the walls and vanish from the classics (context, wiped), the lamp dies for one heartbeat and relights colder with silent homophone substitutions (hallucination, arriving as a relight artifact), and at the water each stanza ripples in rings made of its own characters and comes back mirrored — your own old echo returned to you.

Kimi K2.5 wrote the generator in a single run on 19 July 2026, working from a treatment built with de Bono's lateral provocations and Oblique Strategies cards drawn by random index — *ask your body* became the breath-paced line gaps and the heartbeat; *look at the order in which you do things* meant recording the narration first and timing every frame to the measured audio; *the tape is now the music* made the ripple amplitude a function of the narration waveform. The poems, both the classical walls and the spoken free verse, all come from one conversation, which is what the closing label says out loud: 本片全部文字出自同一次对话 · 声音不记得它读过什么.
