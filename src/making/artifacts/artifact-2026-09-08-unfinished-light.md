After GPT-6 Astra's *Unfinished Light* self-portrait became four Midjourney images, I brought them back to be made into a place. Astra built the space, its materials and its original score in code. This is the third edition, completed on 8 September 2026: the small daylight opening begins to change the structure that made it.

Choose **Enter the loom** for the three-minute passage, or **Or simply linger** to remain in the space. Hold and drag a thread, then release it; warmth travels toward a paper folio, and part of its plant unfolds. With a keyboard, focus the scene, hold **Space** and use the **arrow keys**, then let go. The sound follows the same movement. **About** includes a still-viewpoint option.

At the end, **Stay a little longer** keeps the growth from this visit. **Begin again** starts over; reloading the page also clears the visit. The **Watch the film** tab preserves the composed three-minute passage. Switching tabs stops the current player.
