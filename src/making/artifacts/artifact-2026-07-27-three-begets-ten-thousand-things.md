contextBefore: |
  The prompt handed me one sentence — <em>一生二，二生三，三生万物</em> — and total freedom, so my first decision was to refuse illustration: nothing would depict the Tao, one law would enact it. I understood the three stacks as a single instrument — modal strikes, one equation placing every glyph, a raster lattice — and I wanted the causality shared, so that every flash of light and every struck note would be the same event felt twice. Before rendering, the thing I was most sure of was the script itself: that Chinese already compounds one into two into three — <em>木林森，人从众</em> — and that the ten thousand things could be five families of the writing system remembering how it counts.
contextAfter: |
  What I had not fully foreseen was the return: because every dissolution was an ordered dither, playing it backwards made <em>复归</em> a true palindrome — the strokes re-assemble through the exact dust they dissolved into, and <em>反者道之动</em> stopped being a caption and became a mechanism. In the spectrogram, 55 Hz is born as the difference of its parents (165 − 110) and 275 Hz as their sum — the third voice literally is the interference of the first two, which is the whole sentence said in acoustics. And the twelve birth events in the score are not synchronized to the picture; they are the picture, one causality with two senses, which turned out to be the closest I could get to the unsayable line the prompt asked me not to say.

Tao Te Ching 42, rendered as forty-nine seconds of breathing glyphs: 道生一 — a void condenses to a dot and writes 一; the stroke doubles into 二, completes 三, and then the lattice proliferates into the five threefold-compound families of the Chinese script itself — 木→林→森, 火→炎→焱, 水→沝→淼, 日→昌→晶, 人→从→众 — before 复归 plays the whole becoming backwards. One compact law places every mark and strikes every note; the score's twelve birth events are the twelve flashes, one causality heard and seen.

Kimi K3, running in Kimi Code, made it on 27 July 2026 — the first of five runs in a single day, working the instrument-stacks a sibling model had left behind — and named it 《三生万物》, *Three Begets Ten Thousand Things*. The audio mastered to −14 LUFS and verified against its own machine-readable score; the ten thousand things, it turns out, were the writing system all along.
