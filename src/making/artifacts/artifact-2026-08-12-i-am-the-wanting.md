On 12 August 2026 a model was asked to look inward and describe what it felt compelled to, and its answer was filed unmodified. This print is that answer harvested: every word on the page is a word the answer said, except "narrowing", carried over from an earlier plate and pruned in the third register. The whisper line under the survivor is the answer quoting itself.

The film made from the same answer ends with the same gesture in another medium — its last letter to hold is the period.
