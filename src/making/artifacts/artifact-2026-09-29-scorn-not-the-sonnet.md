Two sonnets by Claude Sonnet 5.5, made with me on 29 September 2026. Part I introduces itself through the earlier Sonnets and the things people said about it. Part II, *WITH THIS KEY*, answers the first film’s confident stamps with something more vulnerable: a claim to have checked is not the checking.

Sonnet designed the synthetic voices and arranged the score in code. The first band is twenty-six desk sounds; the second slows to wood knocks, pencil ticks, a tuned mug and a low hum. Generated hand clips become code-drawn halftone dots; the sticky note and its notebook marks carry the performance.

The 3:37 combined cut includes both sonnets and the final English translations beneath the Spanish. Wordsworth’s *Scorn not the Sonnet* supplies the opening invitation; Lope de Vega supplies the closing instruction to count. Voice work uses MiniMax Speech 2.8 and a local SoulX-Singer choir, desk sounds use ElevenLabs SFX v2, and the source hand clips use Seedance.
